# The floppy drive

`DISK1.wav`, `DISK3.wav` (a page waiting on the disk) and `DISKSAVE.wav` (a patch saved or loaded) are recordings of a
5.25" floppy drive by **ullvieib** on [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/ullvieib/), used under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). In the app they are resampled to
44.1 kHz, levelled and faded at either end (`make_header.py` -> `../vt_samples.h`); otherwise as recorded.
